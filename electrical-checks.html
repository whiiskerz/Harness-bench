Limitations

This tool estimates voltage drop using nominal copper conductor resistance at approximately 20°C. It does not establish safe ampacity, approve a fuse, or certify a circuit. Actual wire ratings depend on insulation, temperature, bundling, routing, terminals, and manufacturer specifications.

A calculation that passes the voltage-drop target does not establish that the wire or fuse is safe.
